How it works

Three steps to zero-touch collections

Set it up once. Every payment after that handles itself.

01

Add your members

Import your member list or add them one by one. Each person instantly gets a dedicated virtual bank account in their name.

02

Set your dues

Create monthly levies, one-off charges, or event payments. Invoices generate automatically on the schedule you choose.

03

Get paid, hands-free

Members pay into their account. We match the payment, mark the invoice paid, and send a WhatsApp receipt — all on its own.
